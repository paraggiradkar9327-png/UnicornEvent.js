4. Your rights, withdrawing consent & complaints

Withdraw consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it — email or call us (details below), or use the “unsubscribe” link in any marketing message. Withdrawal does not affect processing done before you withdrew, and we may keep data we are legally required to retain.

Your rights

  • Access — ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who it has been shared with.
  • Correction & update — ask us to fix or complete inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.
  • Grievance redressal — raise any concern about how we handle your data.
  • Nominate — name another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.

How to exercise your rights

Write to our Grievance Officer, Narendra Atkare Director, at narendra@unicornevent.com or call +91-9921224567, or raise a complaint online to receive a ticket number. Postal address: 382, Trikoni Garden, Shankar Nagar, Nagpur, Maharashtra – 440010. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours. Complaints about your personal data are answered within 90 days at the latest; complaints about online bookings or payments are resolved within 1 month.

Complain to the Data Protection Board

If we do not resolve your concern, you may file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act, 2023. Please contact our Grievance Officer first, as the Act expects you to use our grievance process before approaching the Board.

5. How we keep your data secure

  • All traffic between your browser and our site is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS.
  • Enquiry data is stored with a reputable cloud database provider with encryption at rest.
  • Access is limited to authorised staff who need it, behind a login.
  • Payments are handled by a PCI-DSS compliant gateway; we never store card or UPI credentials.
  • We review who has access and remove it when roles change or people leave.
  • If a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as required by law.

Some third-party tools also run on our site; they are listed, with their purpose, in section 6.
